# Loans at AB Bank Zambia

AB Bank Zambia lends to micro, small and medium businesses and to farmers.
Every application is assessed personally by a loan officer, usually with a
visit to the business or farm. The full cost is given in writing before
anything is signed. The assistant never quotes rates in chat.
