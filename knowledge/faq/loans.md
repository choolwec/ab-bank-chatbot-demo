# Loans at AB Bank Zambia

All loans are subject to assessment, not guaranteed, and depend on
documentation, affordability, eligibility, and approval. Every application
is assessed personally, and the full cost is given in writing before
anything is signed.

## Business loans — Micro & SME

Available to businesses that:

- Have been operating for at least 12 months
- Are within AB Bank's operating network: Lusaka, Kitwe, Ndola, Mumbwa,
  Solwezi, or Chipata
- Have collateral and a guarantor

Applicant requirements:
- Age 21–65
- Zambian, or a foreign national with a business, work, or investor's
  permit
- Valid & original ID (NRC, Driving Licence, or Passport)
- For SME only: a valid passport for the past 12 months, plus company
  documentation
- Proof of residence (utility bill)
- If married, the spouse must be involved in the loan processing
- A guarantor, either in business or in formal employment

**Interest**: 3%–8% per month, loan maturity 4–30 months depending on the
loan amount. [SOCIAL MEDIA TEMPLATE only — the Branch Staff FAQ doesn't
give a business-loan interest figure, only the requirements/timeline
above. For context, Bank of Zambia's March 2024 consolidated bank-charges
publication put AB Bank Zambia's average lending rate at 62.47%–74.97%
annualised — in the right ballpark for a 3–8%/month nominal rate, but it's
an annualised regulator figure, not the same number quoted to a customer
per month, so don't mix the two in one answer.]

**Processing time**: approximately 7 working days after documentation is
complete.

There is currently no business start-up loan product — the 12-month
trading history requirement is firm.

## Personal loans (consumer loans)

Available to **government employees only**.

- Loan range: **ZMW 1,000 – ZMW 500,000**
- Interest: **up to 3.75% per month**
- Tenure: **4–60 months**
- Processing: **within 24 hours** after documents are submitted
- No collateral required

Documents required:
- Signed pre-approval form (available at www.abbank.co.zm/personal-loan)
- Valid proof of ID (NRC, Passport, or Driver's Licence)
- Introductory letter from employer
- 2 latest pay slips
- 2 months' latest bank statement

Refinancing (paying off another loan with a new AB Bank personal loan) is
available, subject to terms and conditions.

Private-sector employees do not currently qualify for this personal loan
product.

## SME Overdraft

Requirements:
- Valid business registration and operating licences
- An active AB Bank account (or, if the applicant doesn't bank with AB
  Bank, recent statements from another bank may be considered)
- Overdraft amount is based on account turnover — typically **up to 30% of
  average monthly turnover** (e.g. ZMW 100,000 monthly turnover → up to
  ZMW 30,000 overdraft)
- Acceptable collateral: term deposit, landed property, or motor vehicle
- Arrangement fee: **5%**

## Guarantor & collateral

- A **guarantor** gives a guarantee for loan repayment, and must be either
  in formal employment or have a running business.
- **Collateral** can include household goods, fixed assets, and/or a
  mortgage (building/land/motor vehicle). Collateral is assessed at the
  customer's premises by their Business Officer.

## How to apply

1. Visit a branch, or request a callback
2. A loan officer discusses the business/need with the customer
3. For business loans, the officer visits the business or farm to assess
   the application
4. If approved, full terms and cost are provided in writing before signing

## Staff/chatbot wording guidance (risk boxes from the source documents)

**On approval**: ✅ "Loan approval depends on assessment once all
documents are received." ❌ Never say a loan is "as good as approved" or
that the customer "will definitely get this amount."

**On timelines**: ✅ "Processing timelines start once all required
documents are submitted." ❌ Never promise a same-day or fixed-regardless
turnaround ("it will be done today no matter what").

These rules apply to chatbot copy exactly as they do to branch staff —
never let an answer imply a guaranteed approval, rate, or timeline.
