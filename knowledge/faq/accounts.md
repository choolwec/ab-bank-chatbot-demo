# Accounts at AB Bank Zambia

## Opening an account — general rules (KYC & CDD)

Accounts are opened at any branch only — there is no online/remote account
opening. All customers must physically visit a branch with:

- Original valid ID (NRC, Passport, or Driving License)
- Proof of residence (a utility bill)
- TPIN certificate
- A completed reference form (see "References" below)

Account opening cannot be completed without full documentation — staff must
never omit required documents or promise to "open first, fix documents
later."

### References

A reference form must be signed by one of:

- A professional (lawyer, chartered accountant, clergy)
- An employer
- An existing AB Bank customer
- A recognized customary authority (chief, headman, court clerk, market
  association chairperson)

For a company account, the reference form can instead be signed by the
customer's church (stamped) or by a company that already has an account
with AB Bank.

### Foreign nationals

Foreign nationals can open an account. They must visit a branch with:

- A valid passport (with visa, per the Social Media Response Template)
- A valid Zambian permit (work, residence, investor, spousal, or study
  permit)
- Proof of residential address in Zambia

## Individual transactional (current) accounts

### Tamanga — Individual Current Account

Fully transactional account, available in ZMW, USD, or EURO.

- Free account opening, no minimum balance
- Monthly maintenance fee: **ZMW 36.00**
- Free cash deposits, free cheque deposits
- Unlimited withdrawals at no fee
- National and international transfers

### Tamanga Plus — Individual Premium Current Account

All Tamanga features, plus:

- Cheque book
- Online Banking (MyABZ) access
- Monthly maintenance fee: **ZMW 135.00**

Opening requirements for either Tamanga tier are the same as the general
account-opening rules above.

## Business & institutional accounts

### Mukula Plus — Corporate Premium Current Account

For limited companies, sole traders/partnerships, and clubs/NGOs/churches/
associations. Available in ZMW, USD, or EURO.

- No minimum balance, free cash and cheque deposits
- Unlimited withdrawals at no fee, cheque book
- Free account balance and statements for ongoing months
- Salary processing, Online Banking (MyABZ)
- National and international transfers
- Monthly maintenance fee: **ZMW 200.00**

#### Opening requirements by entity type

**Limited Company** (all documents original):
- Certificate of share capital
- Certificate of incorporation
- Tax (TPIN) certificate
- Form 3
- Articles of association
- PACRA computer print-out (stamped, not more than 30 days old)
- Board resolution naming signatories and the signing arrangement
- Proof of physical address for the company
- Proof of address and ID (NRC/passport/driving licence) for each signatory

**Sole Trader / Sole Enterprise** (all documents original):
- Certificate of registration
- ZRA tax registration certificate
- PACRA form 3
- PACRA computer print-out (stamped, not more than 30 days old)
- Reference form for the business and the signatory, plus utility bills
- If a partnership: a letter of resolution naming the signatories and
  signing arrangement
- ID of the individual (NRC/passport/valid driving licence)

**Clubs / Churches / Associations / NGOs** (all documents original):
- Tax exemption certificate for non-profit organizations
- Articles of association / constitution
- Board resolution naming signatories and the signing arrangement
- Proof of physical address for the organization
- Proof of address and ID for each signatory
- Reference form for the organization (legal entity) and the signatory
  (private individual)

Staff must never omit required documents regardless of entity type.

## Savings & investment accounts

### Savings Account

Deposits and withdrawals allowed at any time.

- Minimum balance: **ZMW 100**
- Interest: **5% per annum**, credited monthly
- No opening fees, no monthly/maintenance fees

### Savings Plan Account

Deposits allowed at any time; withdrawals are blocked.

- Initial deposit: **ZMW 150**
- Interest: **up to 7% per annum**
- No maintenance fees

### Kids Savings Account

A joint savings account opened for a minor (under 16) by their guardian.

- Minimum balance: **ZMW 10**
- Interest: **7% per annum**
- No monthly charges
- Funds can be blocked for a period of the guardian's choice

To open, in addition to the guardian's own ID/proof of residence/TPIN
certificate, bring the child's document (under-5 card, birth certificate,
or birth record).

### Term Deposit Account (TDA / Fixed Deposit)

An investment account that fixes funds for a chosen term.

- Term: **30–365 days**
- Minimum investment: **ZMW 10,000**
- Interest: **approximately 9.3%–16% per annum** (exact rate depends on
  amount/term — see the TDA interest-rate table; a specific instalment
  quote should still come from a staff member or the tariff guide, since
  the two source documents describe this as a range, not a fixed figure)
- Requires an active Savings or Current account first — funds move
  directly from that account into the TDA

## Joint accounts

Two main types:

- **Joint AND** — all signatories must be present to transact (except cash
  deposits, which anyone can make)
- **Joint OR** — any signatory may transact alone

All joint account holders must be physically present at account opening,
each with full KYC documentation.

## Account servicing

### Bank statements

- **Mukula Plus & Tamanga Plus** customers: get statements via Online
  Banking (MyABZ) at https://ob.abbank.co.zm/landing/auth
- **Tamanga & Savings** customers: statements require a branch visit,
  unless the account is upgraded to a Plus tier (which then unlocks Online
  Banking)
- Any customer can also visit a branch in person, or call the Contact
  Centre on 888 (not toll-free), for a statement

### Account reactivation

Dormant accounts must be reactivated in person at a branch, and the
customer should bring their original ID.

### Sort & SWIFT codes

- SWIFT code (all branches): **ABBAZMLU**
- Sort codes differ by branch — see `branches.json` for the confirmed list
  (Cairo 210001, Chilenje 210002, Matero 210003, Kalingalinga 210004,
  Chelston 210005, Kitwe 210207, Ndola 210108, Chipata 211109, Solwezi
  212813). Sort codes for Acacia Premium/Head Office and the Mumbwa
  satellite office were not given in either source document.

## Common staff guidance (KYC risk box)

✅ Say: "These requirements are mandatory for all customers. Once all
documents are available, we'll be happy to assist."

❌ Never say: "We can open first and fix documents later," "Because you
are known, this step is not necessary," or "You can just ask anyone here
to sign the reference form for you." The same spirit applies to chatbot
wording — never imply KYC documentation is optional or negotiable.
