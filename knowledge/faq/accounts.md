# Accounts at AB Bank Zambia

## Account types

AB Bank Zambia offers three main personal and business account types:

- **Savings account** — for keeping money safe and saving towards a goal,
  with access when you need it.
- **Current account** — for everyday banking: deposits, withdrawals and
  payments, with no limit on transaction frequency.
- **Business account** — for Zambian MSMEs: daily takings, payments, and
  building a banking record that supports future loan applications.
